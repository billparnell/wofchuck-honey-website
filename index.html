Our story

20+ years of beekeeping in Butte County

After more than 20 years of beekeeping in Butte County, we’re excited to now work with other beekeepers here in Northern California to bring you the best raw honey from pristine country.

Many of the hives sit in the foothills just outside Chico, where toyon berry, star thistle, blackberry and other Sierra wildflowers are the main nectar plants.

Beekeeping, for us, is a way to connect to the land and the community, and to give something back.

Mike

Mike kept bees in Butte County for more than 20 years. Today he works with beekeepers he trusts across Northern California, and he’s still grateful every season for what the bees give.

Colleen

Colleen runs our small farm and garden and takes care of everything behind the scenes of the business.
