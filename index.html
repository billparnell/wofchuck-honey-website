Our story

A family that keeps bees in Butte County

Our family cares for hives in the foothills just outside Chico, where toyon berry, star thistle, blackberry and other Sierra wildflowers feed the bees. We place our hives away from farm and chemical sprays.

Beekeeping, for us, is a way to connect to the land and the community, and to give something back.

Mike

Mike has been keeping bees in Butte County since 2001. He offers honey, beeswax and pollination across Northern California, and he’s still grateful every season for what the bees give.

Colleen

Colleen runs our small farm and garden and takes care of everything behind the scenes of the business.

Our honey

Five honeys, five places

Every honey tastes of what the bees were visiting. Our Butte County honey comes from the hives we tend ourselves. The others come from some of the best beekeepers we know, who keep their hives in wild, clean country.
